About

Who we are

R.O.P Health Solutions Ltd is a Nigerian health and development consulting firm, built by senior public-health and development professionals.

We were established on a simple proposition: important health and development challenges are rarely solved by technical knowledge alone. They need sound evidence, strong implementation, capable institutions, practical partnerships and disciplined delivery — brought together.

Our niche is Evidence-to-Impact & Performance Optimization: helping health and development organisations generate evidence and translate it into stronger programmes, better-performing institutions and sustainable impact.

VisionA world-class African public-health and development organisation delivering evidence-based solutions, stronger local capacity and lasting impact.
MissionTo partner across government, funders, implementers, academia, communities and the private sector to design and deliver practical, evidence-informed solutions.
